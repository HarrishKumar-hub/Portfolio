Published Research

Writing for
the field.

Published research on on-device AI mentorship — read by PhD researchers across multiple countries. Built and validated at Christ University, Bangalore. Targeting IJARCCE journal publication.

Preprint · 2025 AI · HCI · Adaptive Systems

On-Device Real-Time AI Mentorship System for Personalized Skill Development Using Screen Monitoring and Facial Analysis

This paper presents NAERIA — a desktop AI mentorship application that combines real-time screen context capture, facial expression analysis via computer vision, and a multimodal language model pipeline to deliver personalised spoken guidance. The system is designed with an anti-dependency architecture to foster independent thinking rather than passive consumption. The proposed framework was validated through user interaction studies and received international readership from PhD researchers across multiple institutions.

Harrish M.K. & Kamalesh · Christ University, Bangalore
